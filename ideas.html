A few short ideasfor Take Root.

Written for Adrienne Phelps by Bhavansh at Code Bunny — as promised. Every idea below is already built into this concept, so you can click through and feel it rather than imagine it.

Explore the concept
At a glance
  • Same words, same story, same pricing — presented more clearly
  • A free self-assessment tool that creates warm enquiries
  • One clear booking path, on every page and every device
  • Journal and FAQ working as search-engine entry points
Prepared for Adrienne PhelpsCode BunnyNovember 2026

Hi Adrienne,

Your current site already does something many coaching sites don’t: it sounds like a real person. Your warmth, your honesty and your “is this it?” story come through clearly — so we kept every word of that.

What we focused on is how easily the right person can say yes. Below are six short ideas, what we noticed, what we built, and why it matters for you.

(01) — Six ideasNoticed · built · why it helps
01

Lead with you, from the first second.

What we noticed

The current homepage opens on a forest photo. It’s beautiful, but a first-time visitor can’t yet see who they’d be talking to.

What we built

The new hero shows you mid-session on a video call, with a “Free · 30 min First Step call” card one tap away.

Why it helps you

People book coaches they already feel they know. In three seconds visitors see who you are, that it’s one-on-one, and that it’s virtual.

See it live
02

One clear path to yes.

What we noticed

Pricing, booking and “is this for me?” answers live on separate pages, so visitors have to piece the decision together.

What we built

Side-by-side pricing (free call · $150 · $390 with “save $60”), a New / Returning booking switch, and a single “Book a free call” action on every page — sticky on phones.

Why it helps you

Fewer clicks between curiosity and a booked call. The package value is obvious, so more people start with three sessions instead of one.

See it live
03

The Footing Check — a free tool that starts conversations.

What we noticed

Visitors who aren’t ready to book have nothing to do except leave.

What we built

A private, two-minute whole-person self-assessment. Visitors rate six areas of life, see their own “root system” wheel, and get a gentle suggested next step.

Why it helps you

It turns browsers into warm enquiries — and their results attach to your contact form, so your first call starts with real context.

See it live
04

Answer the big hesitation up front.

What we noticed

Your best answers — coaching vs therapy, who you work with — are tucked inside the FAQ.

What we built

A “Sound familiar?” section on the homepage and a clear coaching vs therapy comparison on the Coaching page, written in your own words.

Why it helps you

People who’ve “tried therapy and something’s still missing” recognise themselves straight away and feel safe reaching out.

See it live
05

Your journal, built for reading.

What we noticed

Your posts are warm, funny and wise — but they’re hard to find and don’t lead anywhere when someone finishes reading.

What we built

A magazine-style journal with all nine entries, reading time, a progress bar, and an invitation to talk at the end of every post. Coaching topics link to the related story.

Why it helps you

Your stories do the selling for you. Each post becomes a doorway from Google straight to a booked call.

See it live
06

Built to be found and trusted.

What we noticed

Search engines and AI assistants need structured information to recommend a coach confidently.

What we built

Search-friendly page titles and descriptions, Google-ready business, FAQ and article data, fast self-hosted fonts and images, accessible markup, and a layout tuned for phone, tablet and desktop.

Why it helps you

More people find you when they search for a life coach in Raleigh or online — and the site loads fast on any phone.

See it live
(02) — What stays the sameYour voice, untouched

Your words

Copy comes straight from your site and journal — refined in layout, not rewritten.

Your brand

The rooted-heart logo, your orange and green — elevated into a richer palette.

Your pricing

Free call, $150 single, $390 package, $50 late-cancellation — exactly as today.

Your booking

Buttons point to your existing scheduler, so nothing changes for current clients.

Your next chapter

Shall we walk through it together?

Happy to take you through the concept on a short call — or answer any questions by email. No pressure either way.
